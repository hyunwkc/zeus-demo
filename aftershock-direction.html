Aftershock · redesign direction · from three research reports

A small island you watch come back to life

Built from how Mini Motorways, Islanders, Into the Breach, Rebel Inc. and the best bot competitions (Halite, Battlecode, Lux AI) solve exactly our problems. Research files: zeus/research/aftershock-2026-11-01/

What was wrong

A graph, not a placeDark map, dots for towns, no geography you'd recognise.
Everything drawn at onceEvery road, circle and label on screen all the time.
State in numbersYou had to read figures to know a town was dying.
Nothing movesNo traffic, no time passing, no consequence you could see.
A console lookSame family as 911 Operator, the one critics call a chore.

2 · Reading the map

Roads stay quiet and danger is loud. Trouble shows on the town itself, never in a legend.

Pins and a ring on every town in trouble

Up to 3 pin types above the town (trapped, hurt, cold), each a small stack. A ring around it fills toward the deaths it will have next turn.

One danger colour

Warm red, used only for people dying. Everything else stays land and sea.

Roads only when they matter

At rest roads are thin, quiet ribbons. A damaged road shows cracks and cones, a closed one a gap with rubble. The routes light up only while you're sending someone.

Hover for detail

One card with the numbers. The map never shows a table.

3 · A turn

Two beats. You plan in calm, then you watch 6 hours happen.

Beat 1 · plan (paused)

Drag a team onto a town

  1. Team tokens wait in a tray at the depot.
  2. Drag one onto a town. Its route lights up with the arrival time and the odds ("arrives 09:30 · 7 in 10").
  3. Drop it: a bounce and a sound, and a little truck pulls out of the depot yard.
  4. Aftershock zones and roads at risk are marked a turn ahead.
  5. Undo anything until you end the turn.
Beat 2 · resolve (6 seconds)

Watch it play out

  1. The sun sweeps across the sky through the 6 hours; night turns go dark, with lit windows.
  2. Trucks and helicopters drive their routes. A stuck truck stops with a puff of dust.
  3. "+12 pulled out" pops over a town and the people-saved count ticks up.
  4. An aftershock shakes the camera, dust rises, and a road cracks in front of you.
  5. At most one event card: "Bridge at Linden collapsed".

5 · Learning it

One new thing per day. You play by hand first, and the bot comes once you've felt the job.

Day 1One town, rescue teams only. Drag, end turn, watch people come out.
Day 2Roads break. Trucks with water and food; one road closes in front of you.
Day 3Medical teams and a field hospital.
Day 4Snow. Tents and camps before nightfall.
Day 5 →The whole island. Then "write a bot that does this", starting from your own clicks.

6 · Bots and replays

Battlecode's top teams spent more time watching replays than writing code, so the viewer is the product.

The same island, played by your bot

A replay uses the exact animations from playing by hand. Click a truck to see why the bot sent it.

Ghost compare

Your last bot's trucks run faded alongside the new one, so you see what changed.

Your clicks become a starter bot

"Export my day as code" turns what you did by hand into a working bot. First score in under 10 minutes.

Trustworthy scores

Fresh hidden islands each season, many seeds per island, a spread band on the board. Prosperity had to re-run rounds after exploits.

8 · How it gets built

three.js, not deck.gl

deck.gl is built for data maps, which is why it came out a dashboard. three.js via React Three Fiber does lighting, shadows, water, a miniature camera and animation.

  • three@0.187.0, @react-three/fiber@9.8.2, @react-three/drei@10.7.9
  • @react-three/postprocessing@3.2.2 + postprocessing@6.39.5 (ambient occlusion, tilt-shift)
  • simplex-noise@4.0.3, maath@0.10.8; dev @types/three, @gltf-transform/cli
  • All MIT. deck.gl comes back out.

Free model packs (CC0, no credit needed)

Downloaded once, slimmed with gltf-transform, kept under ~1.5 MB total.

  • Kenney Car Kit (~3.4 MB): trucks, ambulance, fire truck
  • Kenney City Kit Suburban (~1.9 MB) + Commercial: houses, shops
  • Kenney Survival Kit (~1.8 MB): tents, crates for camps
  • Kenney Nature Kit (~10.5 MB): trees, rocks
  • Helicopter and collapsed buildings are made in code
